/items.txt has lines of: name quantity price. Write /table.txt with one
line per item, keeping the original order, where each line is exactly:
  the name left-justified in a field of width 12,
  the quantity right-justified in a field of width 5,
  the price right-justified in a field of width 9, formatted with
  exactly two decimals.
Every line is therefore exactly 26 characters. Example: the item
'screw 137 3.5' becomes this exact line:
'screw         137     3.50'
