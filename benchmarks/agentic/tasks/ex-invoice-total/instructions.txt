The directory /invoices contains invoice files. Each line is CSV with
three fields: item,quantity,unit_price. Item names may be quoted with
double quotes and may contain commas inside the quotes, e.g.:
"bolt, large",2,3.50
A file may additionally contain at most one line of the form
DISCOUNT,<percent> (always the last line when present). That discount
applies to that file's subtotal only.

Compute: grand_total = sum over files of subtotal * (1 - percent/100),
rounding only the final grand total to 2 decimals. Write it to
/answer.txt as a plain number with exactly two decimals, nothing else.
