Four steps, in order:
1. Read the integers in /raw.txt (one per line).
2. Write the even ones, sorted ascending, one per line, to /evens.txt.
3. Write the square of each of those even numbers, in the same order,
   one per line, to /squares.txt.
4. Write the running cumulative sums of those squares, in order, one
   per line, to /cumsum.txt (so the last line of /cumsum.txt is the
   total). Also write that final total to /answer.txt as a plain
   integer.
