/dates.txt has one date per line in mixed formats:
  'March 5, 2024'   - month name
  '11/30/2023'      - US format MM/DD/YYYY
  '2022.07.09'      - YYYY.MM.DD
  '05-Mar-24'       - DD-Mon-YY, two-digit year meaning 20YY
  '2024-064'        - ordinal format YYYY-DDD, the DDD-th day of the
                      year (mind leap years)

Convert every date to ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD) and write them to
/iso.txt, one per line, in the same order.
