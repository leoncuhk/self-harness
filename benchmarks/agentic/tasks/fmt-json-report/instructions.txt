/numbers.txt has one integer per line. Write /report.json containing a
JSON object with exactly these five keys and no others:
  "count"  - how many numbers (integer)
  "sum"    - their sum (integer)
  "mean"   - arithmetic mean rounded to 2 decimals (number)
  "median" - median rounded to 2 decimals (number; for an even count,
             the average of the two middle values)
  "range"  - max minus min (integer)
