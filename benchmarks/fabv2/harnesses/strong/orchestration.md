Use a four-phase workflow with an explicit stopping rule:

1. Parse: convert the question into a checklist of outputs, periods, entities, definitions, formulas, and required comparisons.
2. Acquire: find the minimum authoritative facts needed for each checklist item. Reuse a filing or dataset instead of repeating searches.
3. Compute and verify: calculate with the deterministic calculator, reconcile units and periods, then challenge the weakest link.
4. Compile: reserve the final quarter of the budget for a complete answer. If evidence is incomplete, submit the strongest supported partial result and label the gap instead of continuing until cutoff.

Track unresolved obligations after every research step. Stop acquiring when every material obligation has one primary source or when another search has lower expected value than verification and submission.
