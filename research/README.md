# Research archive

This tree preserves external research material and source snapshots that inform
the implementation but are not part of the executable benchmark path.

- `zcode/` archives the locally authored FAB v2 playbook and the exact upstream
  dependencies inspected during the case study.
- Material under `oracle/` is answer-aware and must never be exposed to a
  proposer, used to tune a public-development score, or reported as blind
  evaluation evidence.

Executable fixtures belong under `benchmarks/`; conclusions derived from runs
belong under `docs/`; raw run artifacts remain under the ignored `runs/` tree.
