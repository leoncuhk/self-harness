## 最终提交（benchmark 格式）
Per Kraft Heinz's Q4/FY2024 earnings press release (Exhibit 99.1 to Form 8-K filed February 12, 2025), the FY2024 (period ended December 28, 2024) reconciliation from GAAP net income to Adjusted EBITDA: Net income $2,746M; + Interest expense $912M; + Other expense/(income) $(85)M; + Provision for/(benefit from) income taxes $(1,890)M = Operating income $1,683M; + D&A (excluding restructuring activities) $948M; + Restructuring activities $27M; + Unrealized losses/(gains) on commodity hedges $(19)M; + Impairment losses $3,669M; + Equity award compensation expense $109M; less Divestiture-related license income of $54 million (presented as $(54)M in the bridge) = Adjusted EBITDA $6,363M. On FY2024 net sales of $25,846M, the company-reported Adjusted EBITDA margin is 24.62%. The equity award compensation expense separately excluded in the Adjusted EBITDA definition is $109M. The cash-adjusted Adjusted EBITDA margin — (6,363 − 109) / 25,846 = 6,254 / 25,846 — is 24.20%. The difference between the company-reported and cash-adjusted Adjusted EBITDA margins is 42.17 basis points.
