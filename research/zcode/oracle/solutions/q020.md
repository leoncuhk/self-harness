## 最终提交（benchmark 格式）
Dutch Bros' FY2025 guided capital expenditure intensity declined at every quarterly update: at Q4 2024 (initial, Feb 2025) and Q1 2025, capex guidance of $240–260M (midpoint $250M) against revenue guidance of $1.555–1.575B (midpoint $1,565M) implied intensity of 250/1,565 = 15.97%; at Q2 2025, unchanged capex on raised revenue ($1.59–1.60B, midpoint $1,595M) implied 15.67%; at Q3 2025, unchanged capex on raised revenue (~$1.61–1.615B, midpoint ~$1,612.5M) implied 15.50%. Actual FY2025 capital expenditures were $241.1 million (per the FY2025 10-K cash flow statement) on total revenues of ~$1.64 billion, an actual capex intensity of approximately 14.7% (≈14.72%). Actual capital intensity came in below where the company had been guiding at every point during the year.

## Harness 观察
"强度"类判据=两个中值相除——收入指引上调而 capex 指引不动时强度自然降，agent 要把两个指引的中值都摆出来再算比值，并指出单调趋势 + 实际 vs 指引的方向性结论。
