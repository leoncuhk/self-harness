# q024 — 披露分析：AMZN 服务器折旧年限 5→6→5 与 AI 投资节奏

**题目**：折旧年限变更（2024-01-01 起 5→6；2025-01-01 起回 5）透露了什么 AI/ML 投资节奏？要求给两次变更的管理层理由、Q1'25 折旧/净利影响占全年比例。

## 研究过程（5 次工具调用）
1. `edgar_search('servers useful life', forms=10-Q)` —— **用披露内容当检索词**，直接锁定 AMZN Q1'24（2024-05-01）与 Q1'25（2025-05-02）两份 10-Q
2. fetch Q1'24 → "effective January 1, 2024 we changed our estimate of the useful lives for our servers **from five to six years**. The longer useful lives are due to **continuous improvements in our hardware, software, and data center designs**"
3. fetch Q1'25 → 6→5（subset of servers and networking equipment），理由 "**increased pace of technology development, particularly in the area of artificial intelligence and machine learning**"；Q1'25 影响：**+$217M** 折旧摊销、**−$162M** 净利（$0.02/股，主要计入 AWS）
4. `edgar_search('Amazon annual report', 10-K, 2026)` → FY25 10-K（2026-02-06）
5. fetch 10-K → 全年影响："an increase in depreciation and amortization expense of **$1.4 billion** and a reduction in net income of **$1.0 billion**, or $0.10 per basic share…"

## 计算
- Q1'25 折旧影响占全年：217 / 1,400 = **15.50%**
- Q1'25 净利影响占全年：162 / 1,000 = **16.20%**

## 最终提交（benchmark 格式）
Amazon's Q1 FY2024 10-Q discloses that, effective January 1, 2024, it extended server useful lives from five to six years, attributed to continuous improvements in hardware, software, and data center designs. The Q1 FY2025 10-Q discloses that, effective January 1, 2025, it reversed course — shortening the useful lives of a subset of servers and networking equipment from six years back to five — attributed to the increased pace of technology development, particularly in artificial intelligence and machine learning. The 2025 change increased Q1 2025 depreciation and amortization expense by $217 million (reducing net income by $162 million) and, per the FY2025 10-K, increased full-year 2025 depreciation and amortization expense by $1.4 billion (reducing net income by $1.0 billion). Thus Q1 2025 represents 15.50% of the full-year depreciation impact (217/1,400) and 16.20% of the full-year net income impact (162/1,000). The shift from extending longevity in 2024 to shortening it in 2025 — combined with only ~15.5% of the full-year impact landing in Q1 — indicates that AI/ML-related infrastructure investment accelerated as fiscal 2025 progressed, with the majority of the depreciation and net income impact occurring in later quarters as newly deployed AI servers turned over faster.
