## 最终提交（benchmark 格式）
For fiscal 2025: Marriott's Bonvoy loyalty members booked approximately 75% of U.S. & Canada hotel room nights and approximately 68% of global room nights; Wyndham Rewards members accounted for over 53% of U.S. check-ins and over 37% of global check-ins. Funding mechanisms differ structurally: Marriott Bonvoy is significantly funded by co-branded credit card agreements (multi-year U.S. partnerships with JPMorgan Chase and American Express, plus licensed international programs), which provide fixed payments at contract inception and variable monthly payments based on card usage — a high-margin, capital-light stream outside the hotel P&L. Wyndham Rewards is funded by contributions from eligible revenues generated by members, collected from hotels in the system, and applied to reimburse hotels and partners for redemptions — a pass-through-like structure tied to system revenue. Marriott appears better positioned to translate loyalty strength into durable economic advantage: its larger member base drives direct-booking share across a premium-heavy global portfolio, and its credit-card-funded model monetizes loyalty independently of room revenue, diversifying and reinforcing the program's economics.

## Harness 观察
1. **年份口径是本题主坑**：FY2024 的 MAR 数字是 72%/65%，判据要 FY2025 的 75%/68%——检索必须锚定"最新年报"，且题目明说 "using FY2025 data"。
2. MAR 的表述是 "U.S."（FY25），判据写 "U.S. and Canada"——官方判据对措辞做了合理转写，语义判分没问题；但提醒 agent 引用时照抄原文口径。
3. 资金机制判据是**结构性对比**（信用卡外部资金 vs 系统内收入分成），答案要把两边的原句都点到再下结论。
