## 最终提交（benchmark 格式）
Baker Hughes agreed to acquire Chart Industries for $210 per share in cash (announced July 29, 2025), a total enterprise value of $13.6 billion. Against Chart's unaffected closing price of $171.65 on July 28, 2025 (the last trading day before announcement, per the merger proxy), the offer represents a premium of 210/171.65 − 1 = 22.34%. Per the announcement materials, the combination transforms Baker Hughes' Industrial & Energy Technology segment, lifting IET's non-Oil & Gas revenue mix from ~15% to approximately 30% pro forma. Run-rate cost synergies of $325 million (by end of year three) equal 325/4,160.3 = 7.81% of Chart's FY2024 sales ($4,160.3M) and 325/547.4 = 59.37% of Chart's FY2024 SG&A expense ($547.4M). The acquisition meets BKR's strategic and financial criteria: Chart provides mission-critical equipment and process technology (critical applications), accelerates aftermarket growth through the expanded installed base (lifecycle/aftermarket exposure), delivers substantial synergies, and strengthens earnings durability (improved growth/margin profile).

## Harness 观察
1. **溢价判据的数据路径**：未受影响日收盘价不在新闻稿里，在 **DEFM14A 的背景/公允意见章节**——代理书是并购题的第二必查文档。
2. **同日 8-K 有两个 Ex 99.1**（GTLS 的是终止旧协议、BKR 的是主新闻稿）——agent 必须按"主新闻稿跟收购方走"的规则取文件，CIK 打错一位就是 404。
3. 结构性判据（30% non-O&G）只存在于**投资者材料（Ex 99.2）**的新闻稿之外附件——8-K 全附件清单要扫一遍。
