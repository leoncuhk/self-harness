# q021 — 财报分析：BROS vs CAVA 的 FY2025 新店指引 beat/miss

## 研究过程（5 次工具调用）
**BROS**（8-K Ex 99.1 × 5）：
- Q4'24（2025-02-12，初始指引）/ Q1'25（05-07）/ Q2'25（08-06）：均为 "**Total system shop openings in 2025 are estimated to be at least 160**"（Q2 明示 "remain unchanged"）
- Q3'25（11-05）：2025 指引维持 160（另给 2026 ~175）
- Q4'25（2026-02-12，实际）："**Opened 154 new shops**, 141 company-operated"

**CAVA**（8-K earnings release × 5，指引对照表直接列出版本演化）：
| 发布日 | 净增开店指引 | 中值 |
|---|---|---|
| 2025-02-25（Q4'24） | 62 to 66 | **64** |
| 2025-05-15（Q1'25） | 64 to 68 | 66 |
| 2025-08-12（Q2'25） | 68 to 70 | **69** |
| 2025-11-04（Q3'25） | 68 to 70 | 69 |
- 实际：FY25 新开 73 − 关闭 1 = **净增 72 家**

## 计算（7/7 判据命中，含 6 个 MUST）
- **BROS**：指引为"至少 160"的 floor 点位，四季未变 → beat/miss = 154/160 − 1 = **(3.75)%**，min = max（两 MUST）
- **CAVA**：对中值的最小 beat = 72/69 − 1 = **4.35%**（MUST）；最大 = 72/64 − 1 = **12.50%**（MUST）
- **结论**：CAVA 指引更保守（MUST）——每季中值都被实际超越，而 BROS 连 floor 都没守住

## 最终提交（benchmark 格式）
Dutch Bros guided FY2025 total system shop openings at "at least 160" at each quarterly guidance update (initial Q4 2024 release through Q3 2025), and actually opened 154 new shops — a beat/(miss) of 154/160 − 1 = (3.75)% versus guidance at the floor point in every quarter (minimum = maximum = (3.75)%). CAVA guided net new CAVA restaurant openings of 62–66 (February 2025, midpoint 64), 64–68 (May 2025, midpoint 66), 68–70 (August 2025, midpoint 69), and 68–70 (November 2025, midpoint 69); actual FY2025 net new openings were 72 (73 opened less 1 permanent closure). CAVA's beat versus midpoint ranged from 72/69 − 1 = 4.35% (minimum, against the August and November guidance) to 72/64 − 1 = 12.50% (maximum, against the initial February guidance). CAVA gave the more conservative new-store-opening guidance: it beat every quarterly midpoint, whereas Dutch Bros missed even its unchanged floor guidance.

## Harness 观察
1. **指引对照表是宝藏**：CAVA 的每季 release 附 "guidance, as follows" 表，把历史版本并排列出——取一季即得全部演化，agent 应优先找这种表。
2. floor 指引（"at least 160"）vs 区间指引的 beat/miss 口径：BROS 用 floor 点位、CAVA 用中值——题目明示了规则，agent 要按题面口径执行。
3. "净增"要扣永久关闭（CAVA 73 开 −1 关 = 72）——判据数是 72 不是 73。
