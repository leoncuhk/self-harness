**报表流叙述（3 个定性 MUST）**：计划主要通过利润表 "restructuring and other charges" 行流入当期损益（severance/诉讼/减值三分项）；资产负债表侧通过应计负债（accrued compensation and benefits/current liabilities）挂账，期末余额 $302M；现金支付 $2,004M 冲减应计。报告总额之所以同时大于计划内遣散和 Q3 累计数，是因为该行**还包含**非计划遣散（$294M）、诉讼（$858M）与减值（$3,631M）。

## 最终提交（benchmark 格式）
Intel's 2024 Restructuring Plan flows through FY2024 primarily via the "restructuring and other charges" line on the income statement, which totals $6,970 million, comprising employee severance and benefit arrangements of $2,481M (of which $2,187M — "approximately $2.2 billion" — is attributable solely to the 2024 Restructuring Plan and $294M to other streamlining actions), litigation charges and other of $858M (including a $780M R2 litigation charge), and asset impairment charges of $3,631M. On the balance sheet the plan flows through accrued compensation and benefits within current liabilities, with a liability roll-forward ending at an accrued balance of $302M as of December 28, 2024, after $2,004M of cash payments. The difference between reported FY2024 restructuring and other charges and the plan-attributable severance is $6,970M − $2,187M = $4,783 million; the difference versus the cumulative 2024 Plan cost disclosed as of Q3 2024 (~$2.8 billion, vs. $2.8 billion cumulative at year-end) is $6,970M − $2,800M = $4,170 million. The reported charge base exceeds both because the line also includes broader severance, litigation, and asset impairment charges. Headcount fell from 124,800 (December 2023) to 108,900 (December 2024), a reduction of 12.74%.

## Harness 观察
1. **"reported − plan-attributable" 是两步合成**：报告总额要自己加总三分项（2,481+858+3,631），计划内遣散要自己剥离（2,481−294）——没有一行现成的数字，retrieve prompt 必须按表结构逐项取。
2. Q3'24 累计数（$2.8B）在**10-K 正文顺带披露**，不需要去翻 Q3 10-Q——先全文再跨文件。
3. 员工数对比需要**跨年 10-K**（124,800 在 FY2023 报告）。
