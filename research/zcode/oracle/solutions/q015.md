# q015 — Precedents：PFE 全现金收购 SGEN（2023-03-13）

**题目**：用 SGEN 8-K（2023-03-13）与 FY2022 10-K 计算：(1) 对价×10-K 封面股本的隐含股权价值；(2) 用 12/31/2022 现金+等价物+短期投资与长期债务算 EV；(3) EV/FY2022 收入倍数。

## 研究过程（4 次工具调用）
1. `edgar_search("Seagen annual report", forms=10-K, 2023H1)` → 10-K 主文档 `sgen-20221231.htm`（filed 2023-02-15，CIK 1060736）
2. `edgar_search("\"229.00\" Seagen", forms=8-K)` → SGEN 8-K `d467472d8k.htm`（2023-03-13）
3. fetch 8-K → "converted into the right to receive an amount in cash equal to **$229.00** (the "Merger Consideration")"
4. fetch 10-K（574K 字符）三次定向 grep：
   - 封面："There were **186,789,367** shares … issued and outstanding as of February 10, 2023"
   - 资产负债表：Cash **$319,940**K；Short-term investments **$1,415,130**K；负债侧仅经营租赁+其他长期负债（**无长期债务**）
   - 利润表：Total revenues **$1,962,412**K

## 计算
- Equity = $229.00 × 186,789,367 = $42,774,765,043 = **$42,774.77M**
- EV = 42,774.77 − 319.94 − 1,415.13 − 0（无LTD）= **$41,039.70M**
- EV/FY2022 Revenue = 41,039.70 / 1,962.41 = **20.91x**

## 最终提交（benchmark 格式）
Pfizer announced the all-cash acquisition of Seagen on March 13, 2023 at $229.00 per share in cash (Merger Consideration per the SGEN 8-K filed March 13, 2023). Using the 186,789,367 shares outstanding from the FY2022 10-K cover page (as of February 10, 2023), the implied equity value is $42,774.77 million. From the December 31, 2022 balance sheet: cash and cash equivalents of $319.94 million and short-term investments of $1,415.13 million; Seagen had no long-term debt outstanding at December 31, 2022. Enterprise Value = 42,774.77 − 319.94 − 1,415.13 = $41,039.70 million. FY2022 total revenues were $1,962.41 million, so EV / FY2022 Revenue = 41,039.70 / 1,962.41 = **20.91x**.
