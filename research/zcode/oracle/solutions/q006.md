# q006 — 定量：HD vs LOW FY2024 存货周转天数（DIO）

## 研究过程（4 次工具调用）
1. HD FY2024 10-K（`hd-20250202.htm`，2025-03-21，FYE 2025-02-02 **53 周 = 371 天**）：FY24 存货 $23,451M、FY23 $20,976M、FY24 COGS $106,206M
2. LOW FY2024 10-K（`low-20250131.htm`，2025-03-24，FYE 2025-01-31 **52 周 = 364 天**）：FY24 "Merchandise inventory – net" $17,409M、FY23 $16,894M、FY24 COGS $55,797M

## 计算（8/8 判据精确命中）
| | HD | LOW |
|---|---|---|
| 实际天数 | **371**（53 周） | **364**（52 周） |
| 平均存货 | (23,451+20,976)/2 = **$22,213.50M** | (17,409+16,894)/2 = **$17,151.50M** |
| DIO | 371×22,213.5/106,206 = **77.60 天**(MUST) | 364×17,151.5/55,797 = **111.89 天**(MUST) |

**HD 快 34.29 天；HD 存货效率更高**（两个结论判据）。

## 最终提交（benchmark 格式）
Home Depot's fiscal 2024 (ended February 2, 2025) contained 53 weeks = 371 actual days; average merchandise inventories were (23,451 + 20,976)/2 = $22,213.50 million on FY2024 cost of sales of $106,206 million, giving days inventory outstanding of 371 × 22,213.50/106,206 = 77.60 days. Lowe's fiscal 2024 (ended January 31, 2025) contained 52 weeks = 364 actual days; average merchandise inventories were (17,409 + 16,894)/2 = $17,151.50 million on cost of sales of $55,797 million, giving DIO of 364 × 17,151.50/55,797 = 111.89 days. Home Depot turns its inventory faster, by 34.29 days, and is the more efficient company on the basis of days inventory outstanding.

## Harness 观察
1. **53 周年是隐藏考点**：HD FY24 是 53 周年（371 天），LOW 是 52 周（364 天）——用 365 天统一算 DIO 会得 76.28/112.20，两个 MUST 全挂。财年天数必须从 10-K 的 fiscal year 定义里取。
2. LOW 的科目名是 "Merchandise inventory – net"（单数+net），HD 是 "Merchandise inventories"——同名异写，retrieve prompt 要容忍变体。
