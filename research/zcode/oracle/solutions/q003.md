## 判据对照（7/7 命中，含 2 个 MUST）
| 判据 | 证据 |
|---|---|
| EA：在 PC/主机/移动端开发产品的公司 + 能砸更多钱的大型多元化公司 | "significant competition from companies that focus on developing products and services available on consoles, PCs and/or mobile devices… large, diversified companies… greater financial and other resources may provide larger budgets" |
| EA：与电影/电视/社交媒体/音乐等娱乐形式竞争 | "we compete against providers of different sources of entertainment, such as movies, television, social media, online casual entertainment and music" |

| 结论：TTWO 披露更详尽 | TTWO 四分类+点名具体竞争对手；EA 无点名 |

## 最终提交（benchmark 格式）
Per its FY2025 Form 10-K, Electronic Arts discloses that it faces significant competition from companies that focus on developing products and services available on consoles, PCs and/or mobile devices, and from large, diversified companies with greater financial resources that can outspend EA on development and marketing. More broadly, EA states it competes against other providers of entertainment such as movies, television, social media, online casual entertainment, and music for consumers' free time. Take-Two's FY2025 Form 10-K is more granular: it names specific competitors (Electronic Arts, Embracer Group, Playrix, Playtika, Roblox, Savvy Games, Tencent, Ubisoft); discloses competition with Sony, Microsoft, and Nintendo for the sale of interactive entertainment software (large platform-owning competitors with financial resources to withstand price competition); identifies competition from other software, hardware, entertainment, and media for limited retail shelf space and promotional resources; and lists competition from other forms of entertainment such as motion pictures, television, social networking, online applications, and short-form video. Take-Two provides the more detailed disclosure of its competitive landscape: it categorizes competitors into four explicit groups and names specific rivals, whereas EA describes its competitive environment without naming competitors.
