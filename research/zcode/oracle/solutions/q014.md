## 最终提交（benchmark 格式）
Per the January 26, 2026 announcement (8-K Ex 99.1), IonQ agreed to acquire SkyWater for $35.00 per share in a cash-and-stock transaction (subject to a collar), a 38.0% premium to SkyWater's 30-day VWAP as of January 23, 2026, implying a total equity/transaction value of approximately $1.8 billion. SkyWater's fiscal 2025 results (reported February 25, 2026): revenue of $442,139 thousand ($442.1 million) and Adjusted EBITDA of $53,168 thousand ($53.2 million). Implied Transaction Value / FY2025 Revenue ≈ $1,800M / $442.1M ≈ 4.1x. Transaction Value / FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA ≈ $1,800M / $53.2M ≈ 34.0x. These multiples do not primarily reflect conventional semiconductor foundry economics — a pure-play foundry would trade at a small fraction of 34x EBITDA. The valuation reflects strategic domestic manufacturing value (securing a fully scalable U.S. supply chain) and, primarily, a quantum vertical-integration premium: IonQ is paying to lock in domestic fabrication capacity dedicated to its quantum computing roadmap.

## Harness 观察
1. **"announced transaction terms"类判据全在 Ex 99.1 新闻稿里**（价格、溢价口径、隐含总价值、战略理由四件套），一次 fetch 全拿。
2. 倍数判据的分母用**目标公司最新财年实绩**（2/25 发布的 FY25 数），分子用公告的隐含总价值——"大约 $1.8B" 这种粗数配精确实绩，官方判据给了 ±0.1x 的容差（4.1x/34.0x）。
3. 定性结论（"量子垂直整合溢价是主要驱动"）需要从买方身份+公告用语推理，不是原文照抄——agent 要会做"34x EBITDA 对代工商不合理 → 必然是战略/垂直整合溢价"这一步推断。
